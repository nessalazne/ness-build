STEP 4

Questions you might have

How long until I can use it?

7 to 14 days from your first payment, often about a week. Your first videos go out in week one, you get your GrowthOS login once you approve your sample videos, and the full lead system is live by day 14 at the latest.

What does it cost in total?

$3,500 setup, split into two payments, plus your managed plan from $597/mo for the first 60 days and AI tool costs, estimated above. After that, $197/mo if you run it yourself.

How do I know what the AI costs?

I set up every tool for you. Your GrowthOS shows the cost of every video, image and caption, and each provider bills your own account directly, with no markup from me.

Can I see my leads?

Yes. Every lead from your DMs lands in the CRM inside your GrowthOS, with their answers and status. You can export them anytime.

Do I have to be on camera?

No filming sessions. Your avatar is made from your existing photos and your voice from your existing videos. Your 2016 story and client results land best on real video, so occasional real clips get mixed in.

How is my avatar and voice made?

After your first payment, you approve a short consent for your AI avatar and voice. You approve sample outputs before anything is produced, and your avatar and voice are never used for anything else.

Will it look fake?

Some people can tell an avatar from real video. That is why the mix includes your own footage, and why you approve everything before it posts.

Do I check videos before they post?

Yes. You approve the coming week in one sitting, about an hour. Nothing posts without your yes.

Do I own the accounts and content?

Yes. Every account is in your name, and every video, post and lead is yours.

Can I add more platforms later?

Yes, with no extra fee from me. Adding a platform like X, Bluesky or Pinterest is part of your package. The only change is the posting tool's own cost, which you'd see in your GrowthOS first.

What about my website and logo?

Phase 2. Both are included in your Professional package and start after about 30 days of live content.

Will DMs get my accounts flagged?

Automated replies run on Instagram and Facebook, only to people who comment or message you first, using approved tools. No cold messaging.

Is there a guarantee of clients?

No, and nobody honest can promise that. The system makes sure you show up every day and every interested attorney gets a fast reply.

What happens if I stop?

GrowthOS access pauses. You keep your accounts, content and followers, and you can export your lead list first.

What about Born Again Hard?

It needs its own voice and a different lead approach for a teen audience, so we plan it separately once Lawyers Transformed is running.
