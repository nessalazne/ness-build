Quick answers
When do I get access?
Right after you pay. The email you enter above gets a sign-in link for the hub, and your FX Library page is there with the download, the lessons and the Style Guide.
Do I need the Content Engine?
No. The FX Library works on its own: you give Claude Code a video you recorded and it edits in the effects you picked. The Content Engine is a separate Chrome extension that makes the whole short from a link. You can add it above.
What is Style Builder?
It is the Style Guide page in the hub. Every look and caption style is listed with a sketch and a code. You add the ones you want, press Copy prompt, and paste that prompt into Claude Code with your video.
Can I use it on client videos?
Yes. You can use it on your own videos and on videos you make for clients. You can't share the pack or resell it.
Is it a subscription?
No. One payment. Claude Code runs on your own Claude plan, and I charge nothing per video.
Does it come with chat or live sessions?
The FX Library order comes with the free hub community. Add the Content Engine and you also get a private chat with me. The live setup sessions are part of Live Setup, which is sold on the Content Engine checkout.
