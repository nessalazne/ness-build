Will it run on my Mac or Windows laptop?
Yes, on both. Each one has its own one-command setup, and you get the same course and the same free updates. Mac: macOS 15 Sequoia or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Windows: Windows 11, or Windows 10 version 22H2, 64-bit on an Intel or AMD processor. Windows on ARM (some Surface and Snapdragon laptops) is not supported yet. Either one: 8 GB of memory or more (16 GB renders smoother), about 10 GB of free space, an administrator account, and an internet connection.
