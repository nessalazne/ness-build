How it works

From one link to a posted video in three steps.

  1. Open any link

    A YouTube video, an Instagram Reel, a TikTok or an article. Or type an idea. The Engine reads the tab you're on. If there's no transcript it transcribes the audio itself, and for Reels and TikToks it keeps the hook that already worked.

  2. Approve the script

    It writes the script in your voice at the length you pick: Short, Medium or Long, 45 to 90 seconds. Change anything you like. Nothing that costs money runs before you say yes.

  3. Approve the video

    It adds your voice, your face or no face, the visuals and the captions. Don't like the B-roll or the avatar? Re-render only that part. Then pick Post now, Schedule or Save only, and it posts to 11 accounts on 9 platforms.

Close the panel and it keeps working in the background. If a step fails, Resume carries on from there without posting anything twice, and it picks up a HeyGen render that already started, so you spend no new credits.

What is Faceless mode?

Pick Faceless instead of an avatar and the Engine builds the whole video from your approved script, with no face and no camera. There are eight looks: TSX Animation, Vox Collage, Papercraft, Letterpress, Chalkboard, Voxel Diorama, Stickman and Stick Figure, plus any style you make yourself.

What is Record it myself?

After you approve the script, the run pauses and shows you the clean version to read. Film yourself portrait on your phone or webcam and upload the MP4, MOV or WEBM. The Engine transcribes it on your own computer, cuts the filler words, and shortens any pause over 600 ms to 200 ms. iPhone files work.

The cleaned take becomes your avatar and its audio your voiceover, so captions, B-roll, the review screen, the DMs and posting all run the same as in every other mode. On-screen captions follow what you actually said. It calls no ElevenLabs and no HeyGen. It uses whisper.cpp, a free local transcriber the setup script installs.
