Before You Buy

Straight answers.

Do I have to install anything?
No. It runs in the browser. You sign in at app.digicuratoragency.com and the two studios are in the sidebar.
Do I need to code?
No. You paste API keys into a Settings page and use the two studios. There is nothing to build and nothing to run on your machine.
What exactly am I buying for $147?
Access to Creative Studio and Marketing Studio in your own workspace, paid once. There is no renewal and no card kept on file afterwards. Your monthly allowance is 60 Creative Studio slide renders and 10 Marketing Studio videos.
What does a post or a video actually cost me?
Your own API usage, billed by those providers to you. Writing ideas and captions runs through OpenRouter for cents. Slides and videos are Kie.ai credits. Reading a brand site is Firecrawl. I charge nothing on top and there is no per post fee.
Why is Marketing Studio limited to 10 videos a month?
The renders run on my server and the finished files are stored there, so that part is my cost rather than yours. Sixty slide renders and ten videos a month is what the one-time price covers. The monthly plan does not lift those numbers either.
Do I get the live calls?
No. The weekly call is part of the monthly plan. You do get to message me directly in the community, post in the group rooms and the feed, and take the two course walkthroughs for these studios.
Can I upgrade to the monthly plan later?
Yes. The monthly plan includes both of these studios plus Niche Pulse, Brand Clarity, the content library and the live calls. You can start it from the Billing page in your workspace whenever you want.
Where does Creative Studio post?
Instagram, through your own Zernio account. You can also stop before scheduling and download the slides to post yourself.
Can I pay with PayPal?
Yes. Card and PayPal are both on the checkout page.
